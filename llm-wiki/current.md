- **WGC active-delay smoothness diagnostics (2026-06-30):** current code keeps the sync-first safe-candidate/soft-late selector changes, ownership-safe WGC copy-pool retention, and split WGC source/copy surface budget. The retained reservoir stores source frames and is now sized with 125% headroom for source-above-output cases such as 140/144 Hz WGC input into 120 fps CFR; startup smoothness delay is separately clamped to the configured millisecond target so larger storage cannot inflate the shared audio/video anchor, and the final/cadence diagnostics use the same clamp. With uniform active-delay cadence, the playout layer owns source-above-target surplus; ingress no longer pre-drops under soft reserve or decimation-credit pressure when recent input min windows are at/above the CFR target, while true hard full-pool exhaustion still drops before copy. A regular 140/144-to-120 conversion should drop surplus source frames, not repeat; repeats mean some CFR slots lacked a sync-safe fresh frame because of source spikes, below-target intervals, WGC/DWM delivery gaps, or too-new safety rejection. Before repeating a uniform playout slot, WGC now tries the same sync-safe repeat-rescue scoring as the legacy active-delay selector and logs `[WGC CFR] Uniform playout rescued repeat with sync-safe frame` when it can emit without exceeding residual headroom. Holds with no soft-safe candidate are counted as sync-protected/source-limited lower-bound pressure instead of misleading healthy-source policy repeats. WGC emits split smoothness records plus pool/budget/ingress/buffer evidence (`PoolLease`, `sourceFramePoolBuffers`, `copyPoolSlots`, `poolSaturatedDrops`, `overwritePrevented`, `leaseMismatches`, `accPlaySoft`, `accPlayCredit`, `ingressPlaySoft`, `ingressPlayCredit`, `[WGC CFR SMOOTHNESS BUFFER] smoothDelayDeficit/startupDelayDeficit`, `[WGC CFR SOURCE COVERAGE] coverage/reason/bestEffort`); `tools/analyze_capture_av.py --recording-window START:END` can classify pool lifetime/saturation, ingress double-decimation, source-coverage best effort, and small-but-visible policy-added repeat rates while suppressing false encoder/mux/audio labels. Same-device WGC (`wgc_same_device_capture=true`) is still the preferred low-overhead default; judge roughness from source coverage/lower-bound, pool lifetime, budget evidence, ingress/playout ownership, and sync-safe rescue counters before changing capture mode or audio policy. **Anti-freeze floor (2026-07-01):** the uniform playout grid-anchored target is unclamped, so a sustained encoder-overload grid drift (grid tick lagging wall-clock, resync gated by `outputShortfallTicks < 2`) could push it below the entire reserve and hold every tick — a multi-second hard freeze (`sbfreeze`: `longestContiguousDup=2510 (20916ms)`, 4K120 av1_nvenc). `ApplyWgcUniformPlayoutAntiFreezeFloor` now raises the slot target to `oldest − residualTolerance` only when even the oldest reserve frame is too-new for the slot, resuming source-rate playout against the deepest (max-delay) frame; no-op in healthy cadence, video stays behind audio (sync preserved), and `AntiFreezeFloor=N` in `[WGC CFR SUMMARY]` flags encoder-overload grid drift. See `wgc-capture.md`.

- **Audio/CFR sync:** audio/video tracks must remain same-length and in sync. Settled CFR audio pulls are sample-exact to the current CFR target; `[A/V ZERO DRIFT WARNING]` is a strict audio timeline fault, not a cosmetic diagnostic. Render-domain A/V delay is auto-detected by the product-safe audio-only render→loopback probe, cached only in process memory, and applied only by delaying video; CE must not create `audio_latency_cache.ini`, write measured delays to `config.ini`, or use the removed visible calibration/window-flash path in product startup. WGC treats the measured delay as a video-content reservoir: startup may wait briefly before live output, selects a delayed post-barrier reserve candidate when possible, keeps newer reserve frames, remains at max producer rate through low-water/source recovery, and records realized delay/residual statistics. Any startup schedule offset is diagnostic only and must not be fed back as extra steady-state selection delay because that creates repeat clusters/judder. Live active-delay selection scores strict versus bounded relaxed candidates by timestamp error, repeat-cluster cost, rolling residual headroom, and unstable-FPS window class. Healthy/recoverable/post-stall windows have a soft late target around 5-7 ms; the 10 ms cap is only an emergency boundary, not a quality target. If no soft-safe frame exists, WGC repeats and attributes the hold as sync-protected/source-limited rather than accepting hard-only near-cap video content that can make audio feel late. Final WGC selections are revalidated after index-changing steps against both smoothed selection time and raw WGC timestamp; post-selection sync-risk rejections and raw-vs-predicted residual mismatches are strict analyzer evidence. Severe source stalls open a short source-recovery attribution window so unavoidable reserve holds are not mislabeled healthy CE policy. WGC summaries now also compare actual repeats against a source-limited CFR lower bound; `wgc_cfr_smoothness_not_maximal` is strict evidence when excess/policy-added repeats, CE-amplified repeat clusters, or post-selection sync rejections show avoidable smoothness damage beyond source limitation. Analyzer triage separates true `wgc_av_sync_delay_unrealized`, strict `wgc_av_sync_delay_residual`, `wgc_audio_late_risk`, raw/predicted `wgc_timestamp_domain_mismatch`, post-selection `wgc_active_delay_post_selection_reject`, lower-bound `wgc_cfr_smoothness_not_maximal`, incomplete WGC smoothness evidence, minor `wgc_sync_delay_reserve_pressure`, and strict `wgc_sync_delay_policy_fault`; source-starved WGC runs with bounded avg/p95/late residual and repeats near the lower bound are good-ish source-limited references, but mixed source/policy pressure or over-soft accepted content is still a CE visual/sync fault. Every recording should log `[AVSyncProbe]`, `[AVSyncAuto]`, `[AVSyncApply]`, and complete split WGC smoothness evidence or an explicit low-confidence reason. The decoded `tools/run_av_sync_matrix.py --sync-smoothness-gate` remains the content-level oracle. After the 2026-06-20 follow-up, the previously failing inject AAC 120 fps active-delay encoder-pressure scenario passed with preflight-derived latency, zero marker repeats, strict offsets within limits, app-source `pad=0`, and no underruns; the full multi-scenario gate was not rerun in that continuation. CFR source-clock compensation has an about-80 ms deadband and should not chase ordinary buffer deficit; healthy strict runs stop with `ratePpm=0`, `compDelta=0`, `sat=0`. App-audio that produced pre-start process-loopback packets is strict for bootstrap and must not encode a leading silent head. A process-loopback source that first becomes active late joins at the current track cursor and trims stale backlog; a previously active name-monitored source that exits/restarts or is re-activated carries a new packet epoch, resets every fanned route's local buffers, and atomically uses the same live-join path. Exact final durations are not enough if decoded content or logs show late-source replay. Duplicate source routing is supported, including the same app source recorded to multiple pure/mixed tracks; sparse started app sources contribute source-local silence instead of stalling the exported track, and analyzer triage reports them as `sparse_app_source_silence` rather than strict audio timeline faults when stop-source evidence shows no active ring content. Writer finalization and post-mux probing are separate: mux close must complete first, a probe timeout is bounded validation evidence, and shutdown must not block later captures. Live source underruns (`forceDrain=0`) remain strict unless stop-source evidence proves the source was sparse/inactive; stop-tail padding (`forceDrain=1`) is diagnostic only when decoded content and final durations are clean. Multi-system, microphone, app-audio, codec padding/finalization, and drift handling details live in `multi-audio-capture.md`, `wgc-capture.md`, and `cfr-capture-sync.md`.
- **Process-loopback selection and first-packet proof (2026-07-14):** process-name capture selects the deterministic largest same-executable process-tree root rather than Toolhelp's first matching child.
