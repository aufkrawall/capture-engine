- **Audio/CFR sync:** audio/video tracks must remain same-length and in sync. Settled CFR audio pulls are sample-exact to the current CFR target; `[A/V ZERO DRIFT WARNING]` is a strict audio timeline fault, not a cosmetic diagnostic. Inject CFR source-clock correction and its 60 ms ingestion reservoir ignore downstream encoder/lookahead latency; WGC/DXGI use only their measured screen-content lag targets, while VFR/non-CFR retains pipeline-lag behavior. Render-domain A/V delay is auto-detected by the product-safe audio-only render→loopback probe, cached only in process memory, and applied only by delaying video; CE must not create `audio_latency_cache.ini`, write measured delays to `config.ini`, or use the removed visible calibration/window-flash path in product startup. WGC treats the measured delay as a video-content reservoir: startup may wait briefly before live output, selects a delayed post-barrier reserve candidate when possible, keeps newer reserve frames, remains at max producer rate through low-water/source recovery, and records realized delay/residual statistics. Any startup schedule offset is diagnostic only and must not be fed back as extra steady-state selection delay because that creates repeat clusters/judder. Live active-delay selection scores strict versus bounded relaxed candidates by timestamp error, repeat-cluster cost, rolling residual headroom, and unstable-FPS window class. Healthy/recoverable/post-stall windows have a soft late target around 5-7 ms; the 10 ms cap is only an emergency boundary, not a quality target. If no soft-safe frame exists, WGC repeats and attributes the hold as sync-protected/source-limited rather than accepting hard-only near-cap video content that can make audio feel late. Final WGC selections are revalidated after index-changing steps against both smoothed selection time and raw WGC timestamp; post-selection sync-risk rejections and persistent raw-vs-predicted residual mismatches are strict analyzer evidence. Severe source stalls open a short source-recovery attribution window so unavoidable reserve holds are not mislabeled healthy CE policy. Screen-capture summaries compare actual repeats against a source-limited CFR lower bound; `*_cfr_smoothness_not_maximal` is strict evidence when excess/policy-added repeats, CE-amplified repeat clusters, or post-selection sync rejections show avoidable smoothness damage beyond source limitation. Analyzer triage uses WGC-prefixed names for WGC and DXGI-prefixed names for Desktop Duplication, and uses `capture_phase=2` telemetry to exclude pre-live Present gaps. A solitary 10-11 ms residual maximum is contextual when avg/p95 evidence, policy holds, coverage, and selection remain clean; persistent residuals, policy-coupled late evidence, over-soft accepted content, mixed source/policy pressure, or excess repeat clusters remain CE visual/sync faults. Partial startup reservoirs and delay variation bounded by the worst observed delivered-source interval are context when complete coverage/verdict evidence proves maximal source-limited playout with no policy, safe-candidate, post-selection, or timestamp-domain fault. Every recording should log `[AVSyncProbe]`, `[AVSyncAuto]`, `[AVSyncApply]`, and complete split screen-capture smoothness evidence or an explicit low-confidence reason. The decoded `tools/run_av_sync_matrix.py --sync-smoothness-gate` remains the content-level oracle. CFR source-clock compensation has an about-80 ms deadband and should not chase ordinary buffer deficit; healthy strict runs stop with `ratePpm=0`, `compDelta=0`, `sat=0`. App-audio that produced pre-start process-loopback packets is strict for bootstrap and must not encode a leading silent head. A process-loopback source that first becomes active late joins at the current track cursor and trims stale backlog; a previously active name-monitored source that exits/restarts or is re-activated carries a new packet epoch, resets every fanned route's local buffers, and atomically uses the same live-join path. Exact final durations are not enough if decoded content or logs show late-source replay. Duplicate source routing is supported, including the same app source recorded to multiple pure/mixed tracks; sparse started app sources contribute source-local silence instead of stalling the exported track, and analyzer triage reports them as `sparse_app_source_silence` rather than strict audio timeline faults when stop-source evidence shows no active ring content. Writer finalization and post-mux probing are separate: mux close must complete first, a probe timeout is bounded validation evidence, and shutdown must not block later captures. Live source underruns (`forceDrain=0`) remain strict unless stop-source evidence proves the source was sparse/inactive; stop-tail padding (`forceDrain=1`) is diagnostic only when decoded content and final durations are clean. Multi-system, microphone, app-audio, codec padding/finalization, and drift handling details live in `multi-audio-capture.md`, `wgc-capture.md`, and `cfr-capture-sync.md`.
- **Process-loopback selection and activation-session proof (2026-07-14):** process-name capture selects the deterministic largest same-executable process-tree root rather than Toolhelp's first matching child and records every differently named descendant. Polling/autoconvert is primary; an event-mode initialization fallback must prove a first packet within one second or re-activate polling-only and recycle the disposable worker. Before activation, an MTA `IAudioSessionNotification` monitor is primed on every active render endpoint. The activation boundary atomically records notification generation plus existing render-session PIDs. A newer selected-tree session recycles the worker when the client is unqualified or when no target session existed at activation; the latter remains true even if AudioSes emits qualifying zero-content placeholder packets. A qualified client with pre-existing target-session evidence stays stable, legitimately idle polling stays silent without churn, and arbitrary process-tree growth is not a trigger. Ordered epoch reset treats libswresample's zero-output flush as terminal even if its reported intrinsic delay remains nonzero. Logs distinguish mode/qualification, selected root/tree, session generations/evidence, exact startup recovery, `app-active-no-data`, and never-activated sources; the analyzer does not confuse exact padded duration for captured app content. This is independent of WGC, DXGI duplication, or inject. See `multi-audio-capture.md` and `log/recent.md`.
